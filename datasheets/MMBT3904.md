## Description

The MMBT3904 is a silicon NPN general-purpose bipolar transistor, the surface-mount (SOT-23) equivalent of the classic 2N3904. It is designed for general-purpose switching and amplifier applications, offering a 40 V collector-emitter breakdown, 200 mA continuous collector current, and a 300 MHz gain-bandwidth product. It is well suited for low-power switching (driving relays, LEDs, or logic-level loads) and small-signal amplification. The device is Pb-free, halogen-free, and RoHS compliant.

## Application Notes

- **Saturated switch operation:** For a hard-on saturated switch, drive the base with enough current to keep IC/IB around 10 (e.g. IB = 1 mA for IC = 10 mA). At that drive VCE(sat) is only ~0.2 V. Always include a base current-limiting resistor sized from (V_drive - VBE) / IB; VBE(on) is roughly 0.7 V when conducting.
- **Base resistor and pull-down:** When driven from a logic output or an RC network, a series base resistor sets the drive current and a resistor from base to emitter (typ 10 k) ensures the transistor stays firmly off when the driver is high-impedance and speeds turn-off by removing stored charge.
- **Inductive loads:** When switching a relay or other inductive load, place a flyback (freewheeling) diode across the load to clamp the collector voltage spike below the 40 V VCEO limit at turn-off.
- **Power dissipation:** In the SOT-23 package total dissipation is limited to ~225 mW at 25 deg C (derate 1.8 mW/deg C above 25 deg C). Keep the collector current and VCE product within the safe operating area, and provide adequate copper for heat spreading if operating near the current limit.
- **2N3904 equivalence:** The MMBT3904 is functionally identical to the through-hole 2N3904; the same design values apply, but note the SOT-23 pinout (1=Base, 2=Emitter, 3=Collector) differs from the TO-92 pinout and from many other SOT-23 transistors.
